Why segregation at source matters

India generates roughly 62 million tonnes of municipal solid waste a year. Mixed recycling streams commonly run 15 to 25 percent contamination, and one greasy box, battery, or soft-plastic bag can send an entire batch to landfill. Sorting at the bin is the cheapest, highest-leverage step in the whole system — it keeps recyclables valuable, organics compostable, and waste pickers safe.
