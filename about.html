The problem

Source segregation fails not because people don't care, but because they don't know where each item goes. A greasy pizza box is half-recyclable, a battery is hazardous, soft-plastic bags jam sorting machines — and one wrong item can contaminate an entire batch. India generates roughly 62 million tonnes of municipal solid waste a year, and much of what could be recycled is lost at the very first step: the household bin.

Offline base

28-item SWM 2016 knowledge base keeps it free and private.

Run offline by opening any page, or start node server.js with an API key for live Claude vision and chat. Stack: vanilla HTML, CSS, and JS front end, zero-dependency Node backend, Anthropic Messages API.

Responsible AI

  • Transparency: every result shows confidence and its source.
  • Fairness: rules follow public SWM 2016 standards, not assumptions about users.
  • Honesty: conditional items are flagged check-locally instead of over-claiming.
  • Privacy: demo photos stay in-browser, history stays on-device.
  • Scope safety: the assistant redirects off-topic questions and avoids medical or legal overreach.
